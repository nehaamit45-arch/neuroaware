What is autism?

Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental condition. Autistic people may communicate, interact, learn, or experience sensory information in ways that differ from non-autistic people. Every person is an individual.

🗣️

Communication

Some people prefer direct language, extra processing time, written communication, or alternative ways to communicate. There is no single way all autistic people communicate.

🎧

Sensory experiences

Sounds, lights, textures, smells, or busy environments can feel different from person to person. A calmer environment or suitable adjustments may help.

🔁

Routines and interests

Predictability and focused interests can be important to some autistic people. These traits alone do not tell you whether someone is autistic.

🤝

How to be supportive

Listen to the person, respect their communication preferences, avoid assumptions, and ask what support works for them.

🩺

Assessment

Only appropriately qualified professionals can carry out a clinical assessment. A website quiz cannot confirm or rule out autism.

Respect firstAutism is not something to judge from appearance. Avoid treating a person's identity, abilities, or support needs as a stereotype.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A student awareness project

Designed to make learning interactive and accessible.

🎯

Project goal

Share basic autism information and demonstrate how a website can change its next question based on a visitor's choice.

⚙️

How it works

This version is a front-end demonstration made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It uses branching rules—not a trained machine-learning model yet.

🔐

Privacy

This demo does not ask for names or contact details and does not transmit answers. Do not enter private medical information into a public demonstration.

🧭

Next development step

A real ML backend would need an appropriate, ethically sourced dataset, careful validation, privacy safeguards, and professional review before any screening claims could be made.
